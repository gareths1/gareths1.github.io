## Why computer science and history

Studying history, and ancient history in particular, taught me to work from
evidence, weigh sources, and build an argument. Those are the same habits I
use when debugging code or analyzing a system. I'm curious about how people,
cultures, and events shaped the world we live in, and I like finding where
that kind of thinking overlaps with technology.
